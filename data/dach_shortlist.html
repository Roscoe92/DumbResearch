Peak Two · DACH Buy-and-Build Screen

Best independent acquisition targets across seven sectors

The highest-scoring niches from the value-chain screen, each with verified founder- and family-owned DACH companies that are genuinely acquirable — not PE-owned, listed, or already inside a roll-up. Every figure and company is web-sourced; consolidators and captives were deliberately screened out.

Method. Niches ranked on a two-axis screen — Attractiveness (recurring revenue, fragmentation, regulatory demand, scale, margin, growth) × Actionability (consolidation headroom, gated by an investability check). Shown here: the top six niches per sector, favouring the Act-now / freely-acquirable quadrant. Named companies were independence-checked against Handelsregister/Impressum and press; anything PE-, insurer-, utility-, OEM- or consolidator-owned was dropped.  Link-liveness was validated via web search, not HTTP crawl — confirm in diligence.
